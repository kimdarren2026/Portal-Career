## Rules

- Only **approved** assets belong here. Do not copy arbitrary prototype screenshots into this
  directory — Stitch `screen.png` files stay with their screen under `../stitch/`.
- Do not copy assets from here into production frontend asset folders until the asset and the
  frontend stack are both approved.
- Colour and typography tokens are not assets; they live in `../stitch/design-system/DESIGN.md`.
